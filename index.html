About

I'm a 4th-year Biomedical Engineering student at the University of British Columbia (graduating 2027), after starting my engineering degree at Queen's University. My work sits where mechanical design meets the wet lab: I CAD and build the equipment, then culture the cells that go in it.

I co-direct UBC's iGEM synthetic biology team, where our projects have won two Gold Medals and the Best Hardware award at the international competition.
